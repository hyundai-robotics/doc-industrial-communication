### 1.2.2 Connector

<br>

**Use connectors and cables suitable for the industrial communication method.**

{% hint style="warning" %}

**\[Caution]**:  Install the connector correctly by following the procedures, wiring instructions, and installation methods described in this manual.   

   Improper installation, such as inserting the connector in the wrong orientation or applying excessive force, may result in product damage or communication failure.   
   Before installation, be sure to verify the connector orientation, pin assignment, and wiring.   

   For detailed information on the selection, wiring, and installation of DeviceNet and CC-Link cables and connectors, refer to the official installation guidelines provided by the respective network organizations.   
   - DeviceNet: ODVA, DeviceNet Cable System - Planning and Installation    
   - CC-Link: CC-Link Partner Association (CLPA), CC-Link Cable Wiring Manual    

{% endhint %}
